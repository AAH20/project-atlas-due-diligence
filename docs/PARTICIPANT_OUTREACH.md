## Suggested maintainer-approved invitation

We are running a free, kudos-only, one-month Frontier Due Diligence Challenge organized by Apex Growth Systems LLC. Builders retain their original work; no purchase or commercial IP assignment is required. The optional Project Atlas v0.1 pack provides fully synthetic acquisition evidence, public worked answers and reproducible accounting checks. It is a teaching set with explicit realism limits, not a held-out frontier benchmark. We welcome contributions on evidence integrity, financial reconciliation, privacy preparation, document ETL and staged evidence updates.
